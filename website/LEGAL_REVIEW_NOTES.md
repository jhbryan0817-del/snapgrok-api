# Legal review notes for Zenaian

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Whop checkout terms in this release
are product-aligned working drafts, not a substitute for advice from counsel
licensed for the operator and launch markets.

## Required before commercial launch

1. Add the operator's complete legal name, business form, physical or service
   address, and a dedicated legal/privacy contact.
2. Select governing law and dispute venue after counsel reviews where the
   operator is established and where subscriptions will be sold.
3. Confirm age eligibility, parental-consent flow, tax registrations,
   auto-renewal disclosures, cancellation flow, and digital-content withdrawal
   rules for every launch market.
4. Confirm that Whop checkout displays price, monthly renewal, cancellation,
   no-trial, and refund terms immediately before purchase and captures any
   required express consent to immediate digital delivery.
5. Confirm retention periods and data-processing agreements for Clerk, Render,
   PostgreSQL hosting, xAI, Whop, and any monitoring or email provider added
   before launch.
6. Match the Chrome Web Store privacy disclosures and permission justifications
   to the final extension behavior. User-facing disclosure must cover visible
   tab or selected-area screenshots, optional custom context, authentication,
   and transmission to Zenaian and xAI.
7. Review whether a privacy representative, data-protection officer, cookie
   consent mechanism, regional privacy notice, or consumer cancellation notice
   is required in any target jurisdiction.

Whop's current terms describe Zenaian as the supplier and Whop as payment
processor/merchant of record for payment settlement. They also preserve
refunds and reversals required by law, payment-network rules, fraud controls,
or confirmed payment-layer problems. The checkout drafts therefore state a
strong no-refund policy while expressly preserving those mandatory exceptions.
