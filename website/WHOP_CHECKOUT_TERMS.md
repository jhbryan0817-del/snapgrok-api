# Zenaian Whop checkout terms

These plan-specific drafts implement the approved Whop Additional Terms and
Return / Withdrawal Policy. Replace every bracketed operator or support
placeholder and have Korean counsel validate the exact checkout disclosure
before production sales.

## Plus — checkout product text

Zenaian Plus is a monthly, automatically renewing digital subscription. You
must be at least 19 years old to purchase or use Zenaian. Plus uses generative
AI, including xAI Grok, to generate proposed answers to user-initiated
screenshot captures. AI output is not guaranteed to be correct, and the
service is for lawful, authorized use only.

PLUS ACCESS: Plus provides up to 200 successful question analyses per monthly
subscription period. Digital access starts after payment and entitlement are
confirmed. Unused questions expire at period end, do not roll over, and have no
cash value unless mandatory law requires otherwise.

PRICE, TAX AND RENEWAL: The base price and monthly billing interval are shown
at checkout. Tax behavior is exclusive: applicable sales tax or VAT may be
added on top of the base price. Whop collects and remits supported transaction
taxes where available, and the final amount is shown before payment. Plus
renews automatically each month until canceled. There is no free trial.

CANCELLATION AND DELETION: Normal cancellation stops future renewal and
ordinarily preserves already-paid access until period end. Account deletion is
different: access and unused allowance end immediately, and Zenaian requests
cancellation of future renewal. Deleting an account is not a refund request and
does not waive a valid mandatory withdrawal or refund right.

RETURN / WITHDRAWAL / REFUND: Zenaian does not ordinarily provide
discretionary prorated refunds for unused subscription time or questions. This
does not limit mandatory consumer rights. Korean consumers may have statutory
withdrawal rights under E-Commerce Act Article 17. A limitation based on the
immediate start of a service or supply of digital content applies only if the
statutory exception and required disclosures and measures are satisfied. A
separate remedy applies if the service differs from its advertisement or
contract. Where a valid withdrawal applies, Zenaian will process the refund
within the legally required period, including Article 18's three-business-day
rule where applicable. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] or privacy@zenaian.com. Do not
send full card details.

PRIVACY: Zenaian does not intentionally store screenshot, question, or answer
history. Production AI inference requires xAI Zero Data Retention. Do not
submit screenshots containing identifiable sensitive personal information or
credentials. Whop separately processes buyer, payment, and tax information
under its own terms and privacy notice.

By purchasing, you agree to the Zenaian Terms of Service at
https://www.zenaian.com/terms, the Privacy Policy at
https://www.zenaian.com/privacy, these Additional Terms, and the applicable
Whop Buyer Terms. Supplier: [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME], Republic of Korea.

## Whop legal-settings checklist

- Upload the Zenaian Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and this Return Policy /
  Additional Terms in Whop Dashboard > Settings > Legal.
- Enable **Require terms and conditions acceptance**.
- Confirm that checkout shows the base price, monthly renewal, no trial,
  exclusive tax behavior, immediate digital access, cancellation path, and
  statutory withdrawal disclosure before the buyer submits payment.
- Keep Whop Collects and Remits enabled where intended, without representing
  that Whop assumes every seller, tax, or consumer-law obligation.

## Return / Withdrawal Policy — upload-ready extract

Zenaian does not ordinarily provide discretionary prorated refunds or credits
for unused subscription time or unused question allowance. This does not limit
any non-waivable consumer right, valid statutory withdrawal right,
payment-network remedy, or refund required by law.

For Korean consumers, the E-Commerce Act generally provides a withdrawal
period under Article 17, subject to statutory exceptions. If Zenaian relies on
the exception for a service or digital content whose provision has begun, that
limitation applies only where the legally required disclosure and other
statutory measures have been provided. For divisible services or content, a
statutory right may remain for an unprovided portion. If the service differs
from what was advertised or agreed, the separate statutory non-conformity
withdrawal period applies. Where a valid withdrawal applies, Zenaian will
refund within the period required by Article 18, including the
three-business-day rule where applicable.

To request a withdrawal or refund, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] or
privacy@zenaian.com and provide the purchase email and enough transaction
information to identify it. Do not send full card details. Account deletion is
processed separately and does not waive a valid withdrawal or refund right.
