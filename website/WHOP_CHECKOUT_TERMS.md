# Zenaian Whop checkout terms

These are product-aligned drafts for the Whop checkout terms field. Have
qualified counsel review them after the legal operator, address, governing law,
and launch jurisdictions are known. The text deliberately preserves mandatory
consumer, payment-network, and Whop rights instead of making an absolute
no-refund promise that may be unenforceable.

## Plus — paste into the Plus checkout

Zenaian Plus is a monthly, automatically renewing digital subscription. The
price, taxes, fees, currency, and total due are shown at checkout. Your first
billing period begins when payment is confirmed. Unless you cancel before the
next renewal time, you authorize Whop and its payment partners to charge the
payment method on file each month at the price disclosed for that renewal.

PLUS ACCESS: Plus provides up to 200 successful question analyses during each
monthly subscription period and currently uses Grok 4.3. Unused questions
expire at period end, do not roll over, have no cash value, and cannot be
transferred or combined with another account. Rate limits, security controls,
and temporary capacity limits are separate from the 200-question allowance.
Only one paid plan may be active on a Zenaian account at a time. There is no
in-period upgrade, downgrade, transfer, proration, or plan credit.

NO FREE TRIAL: Plus does not include a free trial. The subscription is charged
when the purchase is completed.

CANCELLATION: You may cancel renewal through your Zenaian account. Cancellation
stops future renewal and normally preserves unused Plus access until the end of
the period already paid for. You must cancel before the renewal time to avoid
the next charge. If the account page permits it, you may reactivate renewal
before the current period ends.

REFUNDS: To the maximum extent permitted by law, all charges are final and
non-refundable, including for unused questions, partial periods, mistaken
purchases, failure to cancel before renewal, dissatisfaction with AI output,
or lack of use. No prorated refund or credit is provided. This policy does not
limit non-waivable consumer or card-network rights, or a refund or reversal
required by applicable law, Whop's binding terms, confirmed duplicate or
payment-processing error, fraud controls, or payment-network rules. Where
applicable, by requesting immediate digital access you acknowledge that a
statutory withdrawal right may be lost once performance begins, but only to
the extent the law permits and any required consent is obtained at checkout.

PAYMENT REVERSALS: A failed, refunded, reversed, disputed, or charged-back
payment may result in immediate removal of the affected Plus entitlement and
return to the otherwise applicable plan. A wrongful dispute may also lead to
account suspension and recovery of valid unpaid amounts.

AI AND ACCEPTABLE USE: Zenaian returns proposed multiple-choice answers. AI
output may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable, and you are responsible
for independently checking it. Cheating is strictly prohibited. Do not use
Zenaian where AI or outside assistance is prohibited, undisclosed, or not
